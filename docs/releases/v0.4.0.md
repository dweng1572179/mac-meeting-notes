# Meeting Notes 0.4.0

Transcription begins during recording, and the meeting workspace makes your words, generated notes, and source evidence easier to distinguish.

- **Transcribe while you record:** native AAC sections finalize about every 60 seconds and upload sequentially. Partial transcripts appear before stop; stop saves the final tails and finishes pending work. This is section-based transcription, not word-by-word live captions.
- **Save progress safely:** text is checkpointed before its audio can be removed. Slow or failed network requests do not stop capture. Interrupted sessions retain pending sections, recover unregistered files, and keep unreadable tails with warnings. Older whole-file recordings retain their retry path.
- **Speaker turns:** new/unset recognition preferences default to Speaker detection. Saved text-only preferences remain unchanged. Speaker labels are scoped to each source/upload; the app does not infer that matching labels across sections identify the same person. Vocabulary hints remain available only in text-only modes.
- **Your notes, AI notes, Transcript:** typed notes stay untouched. You can edit AI notes while retaining their generated baseline, restore that baseline explicitly, and read/search/copy the source transcript. Completed legacy text meetings can refresh their notes and suggestions without inventing speaker identities.
- **Grounded suggestions:** one `gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14` structured response supplies notes plus suggested title, context, category, participants, and transcript topic anchors. Suggestions include supporting saved-source excerpts and require explicit acceptance or dismissal. Unsupported suggestions are omitted with a notice while usable notes and verified suggestions are kept. Mentioning a name does not automatically make that person a participant.
- **Questions in the workspace:** a compact composer, Command/Ctrl+Enter submission, readable answers, and expandable exact sources keep questions close to the meeting. Existing source-size limits remain explicit.

There is no repeated whole-meeting summarization during capture. Transcription, enrichment, refreshes, and questions use your paid OpenAI account; both captured audio tracks contribute to transcription usage. No fixed per-meeting price is promised.

## Install

Download `Meeting-Notes.dmg` from this release. Stop every recording and quit Meeting Notes before replacing the app in Applications. Never install over an active recording or choose Keep Both.

This beta remains ad-hoc signed and unnotarized. macOS may require Open Anyway and renewed audio permissions after an update. Stable Developer ID signing/notarization remains the remedy for the underlying identity changes.

Connect/select Bluetooth devices before recording. Automatic device reconnection, cross-section speaker identity matching, and verified Zoom/Teams participant-name association are not included. Capture warnings measure received/writable frames, not speech intelligibility. Review AI output and speaker attribution against the transcript.

## Verification

See [the evaluation record](../evaluation.md#v040-live-workspace-acceptance) for dated checks and acceptance procedures. Native rotation/recovery regressions and the paid v0.4.0 synthetic speaker-detection acceptance passed: 105.4 seconds, 2 sections, and 41 speaker turns, with a checkpoint before stop and durable notes/suggestions. Desktop and compact workspace flows were also verified. This does not establish real-world multi-speaker, accent, Bluetooth-reconnection, or multi-hour hardware accuracy.
